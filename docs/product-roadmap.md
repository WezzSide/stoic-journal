# Product Roadmap: Stoic Journal

## Phase 1 — MVP v1.0 (Current)
**Goal:** Ship a complete, offline-first Android journaling experience that establishes the daily habit.

- Onboarding with Stoicism familiarity selection
- Today screen with daily prompt and quote
- Guided, free-form, and exercise-based journal entries
- Wisdom library with quotes by philosopher and theme
- Five core Stoic exercises
- Entry history with filters and swipe-to-delete
- Customizable daily reminder
- Settings and about screen

## Phase 2 — Habit Reinforcement v1.1
**Goal:** Increase retention and help users see the value of their practice over time.

- Home screen widget showing the daily quote
- Weekly and monthly reflection summaries
- Export entries to Markdown/PDF
- Search across entries
- Streak insights and gentle misses handling

## Phase 3 — Depth & Learning v1.2
**Goal:** Serve users who want to go deeper into Stoic philosophy.

- Themed reading collections (e.g., “On Anger,” “On Death”)
- Philosopher deep-dive profiles
- Short lesson cards explaining key Stoic concepts
- Quote of the day with manual share (still private by default)

## Phase 4 — Connected Practice v2.0
**Goal:** Optional enhancements for users who want cross-device access and richer experiences.

- Encrypted cloud sync (opt-in)
- Audio guided reflections
- Android Wear companion
- Optional community reflection prompts (anonymous)

---

**Principle:** Each phase must prove value before the next is prioritized. The MVP must demonstrate that users form a daily habit before adding sync, social, or advanced learning features.
