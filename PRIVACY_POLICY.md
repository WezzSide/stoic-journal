# Privacy Policy for Stoic Journal

Last Updated: 2026-07-06

Stoic Journal ("we," "us," or "the App") is committed to your privacy. This App is designed to be **offline-first** and **private by default**.

## 1. Data Collection and Storage
- **Local Storage**: All your journal entries, favorite quotes, and personal preferences are stored locally on your device in a private database. We do not have access to this data.
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to create an account to use the App.

## 2. AI Reflections (Google Gemini)
The App features an optional "AI Reflection" service powered by Google Gemini.
- **User Choice**: You only use this service when you explicitly click "Get Stoic Reflection."
- **Data Transmission**: When you use this feature, the text you enter and a brief history of your last few entries are sent to Google’s Gemini API to generate a response.
- **Google Privacy**: This data is subject to [Google's Generative AI Terms of Service](https://ai.google.dev/terms). We do not store this data on our own servers.

## 3. No Analytics or Tracking
- The App does not include any third-party analytics (like Google Analytics or Firebase Analytics).
- The App does not include any crash reporting tools that send data off-device.

## 4. Permissions
- **Notifications**: Used only to send you daily reminders if you enable them.
- **Internet**: Used only to communicate with the Gemini API for the AI Reflection feature.

## 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App's repository.
